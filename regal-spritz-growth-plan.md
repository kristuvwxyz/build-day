**Regal Spritz advantages already in place:** DTI and BIR registered, **4.98★ from 302 reviews** (Carousell), money-back guarantee if a bottle is proven fake, own website, two areas served (Mandaluyong + Batangas), strong niche brands (Nishane, Diptyque, Jo Malone).

**Gaps against competitors:** no physical showroom; reviews sit on Carousell where buyers on FB/TikTok don't see them; decants are probably not the main way new buyers come in.

## 2. What it takes to reach ₱5M

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Average order value, mixed decants and bottles | ~₱3,500 |
| Orders needed for ₱5M | ~1,430 |
| …over 12 months | ~120 orders/month (~4/day) |
| …in 1 month | ~48 orders/day (needs ads + live selling + resellers) |

There are only two ways to get there: **more orders**, from cheap decants that turn new buyers into bottle buyers, and **higher order value**, from installments, bundles and gift sets. Every feature below serves one of the two.

**Timing:** it's October, the start of Christmas shopping. Q4 is the best quarter of the year for perfume gifting, so build the gift-related features first.

## 3. Features to build, by expected sales impact

### Tier 1: build now (before mid-Nov)
1. **Verified Authentic page for every order.** Each order gets a link with photos/video of *that exact bottle*, its batch code, a receipt-style proof of purchase source, and the money-back guarantee. Buyers can share it.
   *Why:* trust is what grey-market buyers buy. It also turns the guarantee into proof buyers can see, and competitors don't offer it.
2. **Searchable live stock list with on-hand vs pre-order tags and ETAs**, plus a **"notify me on restock"** button (Messenger/Viber/email).
   *Why:* competitors post price lists as images. A searchable list ranks on Google, and restock alerts recover sales that would otherwise be lost.
3. **Christmas gift builder.** Choose 3–5 decants or a bottle, add gift wrap and a card, schedule the delivery date, use bulk/corporate checkout.
   *Why:* raises order value, and corporate orders are the quickest path to big totals.
4. **Installments + e-wallets.** GCash, Maya, GGives/BillEase, card installments.
   *Why:* lets buyers afford ₱10–25K niche bottles. Expect order value to go up 30% or more.

### Tier 2: Q1 (turn one-time buyers into repeat buyers)
5. **"Try, then own" decant credit.** The decant price counts toward the full bottle within 30 days.
   *Why:* decants that sell for ₱400 start leading to ₱8,000 bottle orders. Competitors sell decants as a separate product.
6. **Scent finder quiz.** Questions on budget, occasion, and **"lasts in PH heat/humidity"**, plus "if you like X, try Y".
   *Why:* new buyers are often overwhelmed by choice. The quiz recommends 2–3 scents and adds them to the cart.
7. **Loyalty + reorder reminders.** Points per peso, a birthday decant, and a reminder when a bottle is likely running low (estimated from size and purchase date).
8. **Reviews shown everywhere.** Import the 302 Carousell reviews to the site, and request a photo review after each delivery with a points reward.

### Tier 3: scale (once ~100 orders/month)
9. **Reseller/affiliate portal.** Codes for TikTok creators and resellers, tiered commissions, a dashboard showing their sales.
   *Why:* more people selling for you without hiring.
10. **Messenger/Instagram DM order bot.** Replies to "HM?" and "Available?" with price, stock and checkout link automatically, 24/7.
11. **Live-selling companion page.** During FB/TikTok lives, a page shows "mine" claims in real time and turns them into checkout links that expire if unpaid.

## 4. Sales tactics to run alongside the features
- **TikTok:** regular short videos (unboxing, batch-code checks, "smells like" comparisons) plus weekly live selling.
- **Proof content:** post a short video of each shipment with batch codes visible to show the stock is authentic.
- **Partner pickup point** in a Mandaluyong café or co-working space: a cheap answer to Perfume Junkie's physical store.
- **Retargeting ads** to people who saw product pages or messaged and didn't buy, using the restock and wishlist data from feature 2.
