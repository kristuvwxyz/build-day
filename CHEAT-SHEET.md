# K's Build Day Cheat Sheet ✨

## The 4 habits
1. **Sabihin kung ano ang gusto mo at para kanino**, hindi kung paano gawin.
2. **Tingnan ang ginawa ni Claude at mag-react na parang tao kausap mo.** "Gawing mas elegant." "Mas malaki ang photos." "Masyadong maraming kulay."
3. **Pag nalito ka, itanong:** "Explain what you just did, simply."
4. **Pag may sira, sabihin lang:** "That broke, fix it." Hindi mo kailangang intindihin ang error.

## Ready-to-use requests
- **Find My Scent Quiz + Spin the Wheel:** "Gawan mo ako ng 'find your scent' quiz page para sa customers ko, para matulungan silang pumili ng perfume sa 5 madaling tanong. Ipakita ang top 3 matches with photo, price, and a Buy button to my Shopify. Sa dulo, may spin the wheel para manalo ng voucher. Dapat luxurious ang dating at maganda sa phone."
- **My Money Tracker:** "Gawan mo ako ng simpleng page sa phone ko kung saan ma-log ko ang gastos ko in 5 seconds. Isave sa Google Sheet, and every Sunday i-Telegram mo ako kung magkano ang nagastos ko vs budget, at magkano ang na-save ko."
- **Trust Page:** "Gawan mo ako ng page na sumasagot sa 'Is it authentic?' para sa customers ko: saan galing ang perfumes, paano mag-check ng batch code, shipping and payment FAQs. Something I can paste in DMs."

## How to start on build day
1. In Safari, open **claude.ai/code**.
2. Pick the **build-day** project (repo).
3. Type: **"Let's build"** 🚀

## What to bring (upload to the `bring` folder before build day)
**For the Find My Scent Quiz:**
- [ ] Your product list from Shopify (Shopify admin → **Products** → **Export** → "All products", CSV file)
- [ ] For each perfume: vibe (fresh, sweet, woody, floral…), for whom (men, women, unisex), best occasion (day, night, office, date). A quick list is fine.
- [ ] Your 15–20 best-selling perfumes marked, so we start with those
- [ ] Your spin-the-wheel prizes, e.g. 5% off, free shipping, free sample, "try again"
- [ ] Your Shopify store link and Messenger link (m.me/yourpage)
- [ ] Your logo, plus brand colours if you have them

**For My Money Tracker:**
- [ ] Your spending categories (food, transport, bills, shopping, etc.)
- [ ] Your monthly budget and savings goal
- [ ] Optional: last month's GCash or bank history as sample data, **with account numbers removed**
